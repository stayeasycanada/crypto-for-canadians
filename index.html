How to pick your first platform

If you have never bought cryptocurrency before, the honest answer is that the platform matters less than simply starting small. Every exchange on this page is registered with FINTRAC and legal across Canada, so the real differences come down to fees, how much hand-holding the app gives you, and how big the welcome bonus is.

If you just want to own some Bitcoin and not think about it: start with Shakepay. There are no trading screens, deposits by Interac e-Transfer are free, and you can buy $25 worth without understanding anything about order books. The catch is the spread, which can reach around 3% per trade.

If you want the lowest fees: NDAX charges 0.2% per trade, well below what the beginner-friendly apps take on a spread basis. It is Canadian-owned and built for this market, but the interface assumes you know what a limit order is.

If you want to start investing, not just buy crypto: Wealthsimple gives you commission-free stocks and ETFs inside a TFSA, RRSP or FHSA, and that is where most Canadians should actually begin. One correction worth making, because a lot of pages get it wrong: crypto at Wealthsimple cannot go in a TFSA or RRSP — it sits in a separate non-registered account and costs 2% per trade on the standard plan.

If you plan to trade actively: Moomoo hands you Level 2 data and professional charting for free, which is a genuine saving if you use them. Kraken is the equivalent on the crypto side, with the deepest selection and a long security record.

A word on stacking bonuses: nothing stops you from opening several accounts and claiming each welcome bonus, and plenty of Canadians do exactly that. Just remember that each one requires its own identity verification and, in most cases, a minimum deposit — so read the qualification line on each card above before you commit money you were not planning to invest.
