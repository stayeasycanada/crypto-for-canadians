Wealthsimple fees, honestly

Wealthsimple's pricing is genuinely good on the stock side and genuinely expensive on the crypto side, and the marketing does not make that distinction loudly.

  • Stocks and ETFs: $0 commission. Real commission-free trading on Canadian and US listings.

Is Wealthsimple safe?

Wealthsimple is one of the largest independent financial institutions in Canada, regulated by the Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization, with securities accounts protected by CIPF coverage up to $1 million.

That CIPF protection covers your stocks and cash if the broker fails — it does not cover investment losses, and it does not extend to crypto holdings. Crypto on any platform sits outside those protections.

Tax on the bonus

The CRA generally treats a referral or signup bonus as income in the year you receive it, and any later gain when you sell as a capital gain, where half is taxable. On a bonus of this size the tax is trivial, but the record-keeping habit matters once real sums are involved. Keep your transaction history and talk to an accountant — this page is not tax advice.
