Is NDAX safe?

NDAX is registered with FINTRAC as a money services business and with Canadian securities regulators, holds client CAD at a Canadian bank, and keeps the majority of crypto in cold storage. It has operated since 2018 without a publicly reported breach.

The usual two caveats. Crypto held on an exchange is not covered by CDIC insurance — if the exchange fails, you are a creditor, not an insured depositor. And the platform's regulatory standing says nothing about the asset's volatility, which is severe.

Tax on the bonus

The CRA generally treats a referral or signup bonus as income in the year you receive it, and any later gain when you sell as a capital gain, where half is taxable. On a bonus of this size the tax is trivial, but the record-keeping habit matters once real sums are involved. Keep your transaction history and talk to an accountant — this page is not tax advice.
