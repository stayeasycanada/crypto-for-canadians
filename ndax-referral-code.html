NDAX fees, honestly

Fees are where NDAX earns its reputation, and it is worth understanding why the comparison is not as simple as the headline number suggests.

  • Trading: 0.2% per trade, both maker and taker. On a $1,000 buy that is $2.
  • CAD deposits: free by Interac e-Transfer, bank wire or EFT.
  • CAD withdrawals: $4.99 flat by e-Transfer. Worth knowing before you plan to move money in and out often.
  • Crypto withdrawals: network fees, which vary by coin and by how busy the network is.

The number to compare against is not another exchange's stated commission but its spread. Apps marketed as commission-free generally make their money by quoting you a slightly worse price than the market one — often 1% to 2.5% in effect. That is why a 0.2% explicit fee usually works out cheaper overall, and why the gap widens the more you buy.

Is NDAX safe?

NDAX is registered with FINTRAC as a money services business and with Canadian securities regulators, holds client CAD at a Canadian bank, and keeps the majority of crypto in cold storage. It has operated since 2018 without a publicly reported breach.

Two things to be clear about, though. Crypto held on any exchange is not covered by CDIC insurance the way cash in a chequing account is — if the exchange fails, you are a creditor. And the regulatory standing of the platform says nothing about the asset: Bitcoin has repeatedly lost more than half its value within a year. Those are separate risks and the first one being handled does not help with the second.

Do I pay tax on the NDAX bonus?

Probably, yes. The CRA generally treats a referral or signup bonus as income in the year you receive it, and any later gain when you sell crypto as a capital gain, where half the gain is taxable. On $15 the amount at stake is trivial, but the record-keeping habit matters once you are trading real sums. Keep your transaction history and speak to an accountant — this page is not tax advice.
